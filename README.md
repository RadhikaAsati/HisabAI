# HisabAI

### Your Shop. Your Numbers. Your Language.

HisabAI is a cash-aware AI business companion designed to help small
shopkeepers make informed purchasing and inventory decisions.

## Features

- Cash-aware smart purchase planning
- Inventory and stock management
- Business bookkeeping assistance
- AI-powered business insights

> Features are under active development.

## Tech Stack

- Backend: Python, FastAPI
- Database: PostgreSQL
- ORM: SQLAlchemy
- AI: Gemini API (planned)
- Testing: Pytest
